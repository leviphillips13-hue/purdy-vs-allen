Games lost from winning positions

Losses in which his team's win probability reached 75% or more in the second half. The verdict weighs only his own interceptions, lost fumbles and sacks after that peak: QB-driven at 20 WP points or more combined, or any turnover in overtime; shared at 5 to 20; otherwise not on him.

Wins from losing positions

Wins in which his team's win probability fell to 25% or less in the second half, with the WP he added on his own plays from that low point on.

How this was measured

Plays counted
Every dropback (passes, sacks, scrambles) and designed run where he is the play's quarterback. Kneels, spikes and two-point tries are left out. Only games where he had at least 10 dropbacks count.
EPA and CPOE
nflverse's expected points added, credited to the quarterback (qb_epa), and completion percentage over expected on pass attempts.
Win probability added
The change in his team's chance of winning on each play, from the nflverse model. Overtime is excluded: the model is unreliable there. It put San Francisco at 99% before a field goal in the Super Bowl LVIII overtime, and it credited Allen's game-losing overtime interception at Denver with a gain.
Miscues
An interception he threw, a fumble he lost, or a sack he took. A costly miscue cost his team 10 WP points or more on one play.
Late and close
Fourth quarter and overtime with the score within 8 points. Trailing late means down by 1 to 8 in the same window.
Ranks
Among quarterbacks with 250 or more dropbacks in that season (75 for 2026 so far), all games included.
